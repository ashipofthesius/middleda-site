title: A good day date: 2026-11-10 time: 18:00 ---

In your twenties a good day has to have something in it. A win, a trip, a little money, some news worth repeating. An ordinary Tuesday doesn't count.

Somewhere along the way the list gets shorter, and at fifty-three mine reads about like this. Everybody's home. Nothing's broken, not the truck, not the air conditioner, not anybody's bones. Supper was good. Nobody called with bad news.

Anybody who has sat in a hospital waiting room at two in the morning, or stood in a receiving line at a funeral home, or waited up for a kid who should have been home an hour ago, knows what an uneventful evening is worth. You quit asking a day to prove something to you and start noticing what it handed you.

Nothing hurts that didn't hurt yesterday. That's a good day.
