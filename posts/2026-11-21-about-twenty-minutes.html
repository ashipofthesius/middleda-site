title: About twenty minutes date: 2026-11-21 time: 09:00 ---

Saturday is the day for fixing things that broke. But it ain't really that simple. First there's the buildup. I get asked to fix 'x'. But let's not get too hasty, because first I have to figure out whether my wife is serious about this purported fixing. If I'm asked just once, I figure she isn't really serious about it.

If I'm asked and asked and asked again, that's how I can tell it really needs fixing. And the harder it is to fix, well then, the more times I should be asked. That makes perfect sense. If the fixing is going to take time and attention, then the asking should take time and attention too.

But supposing she's dedicated to the ask, and thus I am committed. How long will it take? The answer is always "about twenty minutes." Now, it never takes about twenty minutes. But since I have no idea how long it will take, that's the answer I give, and it makes me seem reasonable.

Usually the fixing takes the entire Saturday. One simple job becomes three trips to the hardware store and a new tool I didn't know I needed but which is absolutely vital for the job.

There's a 50-50 chance that after four or five hours I declare it can't be fixed and a repairman must be summoned. But it is incomprehensible that I would return my new tool, since something else may need fixing later.
