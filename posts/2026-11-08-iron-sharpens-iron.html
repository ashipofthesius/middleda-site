title: Iron sharpens iron date: 2026-11-08 time: 08:15 ---

"Iron sharpeneth iron; so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend." Proverbs 27:17.

Anybody who has ever put an edge on a knife knows that verse isn't describing something gentle. Iron doesn't sharpen iron by resting against it. There's pressure, there's friction, and there's a sound that makes you wince. Something gets ground off. The blade comes away thinner and hotter than it went in, and it cuts better because of it.

That's what the verse says a friend is for.

Most of us pick friends who are easy to be around, and there's nothing wrong with that. But the friend the proverb has in mind is the one who asks what you're doing about the problem you've been talking about for a year, and who notices you've gotten comfortable and says so. It isn't pleasant, and it isn't supposed to be. A blade that never touches the stone goes dull, and a dull blade is the one that slips and cuts you.

You're somebody's iron. God didn't make any of us to stay sharp alone, and if the people who count on you only ever hear that they're doing fine, you've quit being a friend and started being company.
