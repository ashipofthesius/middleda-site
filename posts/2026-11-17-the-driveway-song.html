title: The song you won’t turn off in the driveway date: 2026-11-17 time: 18:00 ---

There’s a song you won’t turn off when you pull into the driveway. You’re home, the groceries are sitting in the back seat getting warmer by the minute, and you sit there anyway until it’s over, because some songs still deserve the last three minutes.

Most of us have one. Mine is “We Built This City” by Starship.

I grew up in the 80s. Don’t judge me. It wasn’t until college that I found out what a ‘hoopla’ was. I just thought it was a catchy word they made up.

But look, I know what folks say about the song. Rolling Stone readers voted it the worst song of the 80s. Coincidentally, more people have heard the song than read Rolling Stone magazine. It still went to number one, and I’ve never managed to outgrow it.

I don’t think anybody really chooses these songs from our childhood when life was simpler. It just so happens mine was filled with big hair bands.

So if you have a favorite childhood song, tell me in the comments. But this is a safe space. Unless you like emo. Then, not so much.
