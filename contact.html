Open Records

Investigator Rocky Davis is the open records official for this office. He may be reached via email at jrdavis@pacga.org or at the Lyons Office at (912) 526-5211. Requests under the Georgia Open Records Act may be directed to him. Closed-case dispositions are public record.

If you are in danger

Call 911. This office prosecutes cases; emergencies belong with your local sheriff's office or police department first.
