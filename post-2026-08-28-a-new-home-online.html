A new home for the office online

August 28, 2026

This office has rebuilt its website. The address and phone number are current, the biography is current, and two things are new.

First, the office's closed-case numbers are now published on The Record. Closed cases are public record, and the people of this circuit are entitled to see what their District Attorney's office does with its docket.

Second, the documents people actually ask for are gathered in one place: the office's Use of Force Protocol, the Early Intervention Program guide and forms, and record restriction paperwork, all on Files & Policies. Open records requests go to Investigator Rocky Davis, and his contact information is on the Contact page.

Updates from the office will post here.
