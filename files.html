Early Intervention Program

The Early Intervention Program is this office's diversion program under O.C.G.A. § 15-18-80. It routes eligible lower-level cases into supervision, restitution, and accountability instead of a felony record; completing the program resolves the case. The current program guide and forms:

  • Form 1 — Risk Assessment
  • Form 2 — Rights & Consent
